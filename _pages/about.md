I am a Research Assistant at Elite Research Lab LLC in Dhaka, Bangladesh, where I support ongoing research through literature review, data collection and analysis, and documentation of findings. I hold a B.Sc. in Physics from Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University (HSTU), Dinajpur, and I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Applied Statistics & Data Science at Jahangirnagar University. Alongside this, I work as a data scientist and AI developer, having spent recent years teaching physics, mathematics, and programming, and delivering freelance data-analysis work — most notably in healthcare revenue-cycle management.

My research interests sit at the intersection of physics and AI: I am working to bridge physics-based reasoning with machine learning, deep learning, vision-language models, and multimodal AI more broadly. This direction has taken shape through applied work — building an LLM- and RAG-based pipeline to analyze labor-market trends from Bangladeshi job boards (BDJobs, Chakri), and a data-quality investigation across two healthcare EHR datasets that traced discrepancies to non-standard billing codes and a system synchronization failure. I am now deepening my foundation in deep learning to move from applied data science toward more physics-informed AI research.
